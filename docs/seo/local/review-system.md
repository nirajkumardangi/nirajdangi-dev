# Client Review Acquisition & Reputation System

This document outlines the ethical, white-hat client review acquisition process for **niraj.online**. 

---

## 1. Compliance & Ethical Boundaries (Hard Rules)

1. **Zero Fake Reviews**: Never create synthetic reviews, ask friends/family who did not hire you, or purchase reviews from third-party networks. Google detects IP clusters and flags fraudulent reviews, resulting in permanent GBP suspension.
2. **Zero Review Gating**: You cannot ask clients "Did you like our service?" and only send the Google review link to happy clients while hiding it from others. All clients must receive equal access to share feedback.
3. **No Incentives**: Offering discounts, cash rewards, or free services in exchange for positive reviews violates Google's Terms of Service and Indian consumer protection guidelines.
4. **Healthcare Ethics**: Never solicit or publish patient testimonials or clinical ratings for doctor websites, in strict compliance with the National Medical Commission (NMC) regulations.

---

## 2. Review Timing & Workflow

The highest conversion rate for authentic reviews occurs **7 to 10 days after production launch**:
- Day 0: Production deployment and final code handover.
- Day 7: Check-in message to ensure everything is operating smoothly and initial customer enquiries are arriving.
- Day 8–10: Send the formal review request via WhatsApp or Email.

## 4. How to Generate Your Direct Google Review Shortlink

1. Log into your verified Google Business Profile.
2. Under "Your business on Google", click **Ask for reviews**.
3. Copy the generated short URL (format: `https://g.page/r/[ID]/review`).
4. Keep this link pinned in your WhatsApp business quick-replies.

---

## 5. Responding to Every Review (SEO Advantage)

Google rewards active profiles where the owner responds to 100% of reviews within 24–48 hours. Responses should naturally incorporate localized context and specific deliverables.

### Sample Response to a 5-Star Review:
> *"Thank you so much for your feedback, [Client Name]! It was wonderful collaborating on the custom Next.js web platform for [Business Name]. Ensuring fast mobile speeds and instant WhatsApp booking for customers in Ranchi was a top priority. Wishing your business continued growth, and I look forward to supporting you!"*

### Sample Response to a Critical / Issue Review:
> *"Hi [Client Name], thank you for sharing your feedback. I take pride in clear communication and reliable code. I have reached out directly via WhatsApp to review the items you mentioned and ensure your system performs exactly as expected. Thank you for helping me improve."*
