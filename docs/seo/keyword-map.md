## 2. Cannibalization Guardrails (Strict One-URL Mapping)
To prevent keyword cannibalization across national, regional, and local landing pages:
1. `website developer in ranchi` $\rightarrow$ Exclusively targets `/website-developer-ranchi`. The homepage (`/`) targets brand identity and overall studio positioning (`Websites for Doctors, Creators & Event Brands`).
2. `web development jharkhand` $\rightarrow$ Exclusively targets `/website-developer-jharkhand`.
3. `hire full stack developer india` $\rightarrow$ Exclusively targets `/hire-developer-india`.
4. Specific vertical terms (e.g., `doctor website design`, `clinic appointment booking`) $\rightarrow$ Exclusively target the respective `/solutions/*` page, not the `/work/*` case study. Case studies target specific brand names and technical implementation queries (`rovala-in case study`, `ai knowledge assistant chroma rag`).
